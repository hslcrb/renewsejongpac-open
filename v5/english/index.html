Plan your performance visit

  1. 1

    Choose a performance

    Check the date, time, venue, age restrictions and language information on the individual listing.

  2. 2

    Book through the official service

    The official English booking guide describes email verification, date and seat selection, and payment. Online booking usually closes two hours before the performance; exceptions apply.

  3. 3

    Check collection and cancellation rules

    Use the instructions supplied with your confirmed booking. An enquiry or saved request does not issue a ticket.

Accessibility and assistance

The official accessibility guide describes tactile guidance, assistance call buttons, wheelchair lifts and elevators. Contact the centre before your visit to confirm a suitable entrance and assistance for your venue.

For current ticket availability and confirmed booking information, follow the official English links below.
